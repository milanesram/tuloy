# Tuloy

**Your story continues. Your next chapter starts here.**

In Tagalog, *tuloy* expresses continuation. Said as a welcome, it invites someone inside. For people building a career in a new country, these meanings hold together the experience they bring and the possibilities ahead. [Dictionary entry](https://diksiyonaryo.ph/word/tuloy).

Tuloy is a place to gather that experience, examine opportunities, and take the next step with purpose.

**Bring your experience. Step into what comes next.**

The mark is an open doorway crossed by an unbroken forward path. Teal signals the primary actions; warm neutral surfaces and editorial serif headings make the workspace feel welcoming. Status and eligibility remain expressed in words, independently of brand color.

Use a capable, welcoming, practical voice. Explain transferable experience precisely. Distinguish supported evidence from unknown requirements. Avoid guarantees, automatic application claims, or assumptions about the level at which someone should restart.

The product is independent of other organizations using Tuloy. The word is established and not an exclusive invention. The proposed repository name is not a domain or trademark clearance.
